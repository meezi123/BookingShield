# BookingShield: Problem Definition

## Business problem

Hotel cancellations create lost revenue, unused rooms, and planning problems.

## Prediction objective

At booking creation, predict whether a booking is likely to be cancelled.

## Target

is_canceled

- 1: booking was cancelled
- 0: booking was not cancelled

## Unit of prediction

One row represents one hotel booking.

## Stakeholders

Hotel revenue manager and reservations team.

## Business action

Use cancellation risk to prioritize booking confirmation, guest follow-up, deposit requests, and revenue-recovery actions.
